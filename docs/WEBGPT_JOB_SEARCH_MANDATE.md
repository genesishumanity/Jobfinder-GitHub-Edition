# WebGPT Job Search Mandate

## Objective
Optimize Job Finder for qualified remote interview opportunities, not title prestige or raw listing volume.

## Professional profile
Multidisciplinary creative professional at the intersection of creative thinking, visual and film production, hands-on execution, AI-assisted creative workflows, and systems thinking. Experience spans filmmaking/design, integrated creative work, and Creative Lead responsibility. Strengths include concept development, treatments/storyboards, pitching, creative production, film/edit/design collaboration, rapid experimentation, and AI-assisted creative/product systems.

Do not assume deep traditional-agency tenure, long-term media-budget ownership, senior software engineering, or specialist art-direction craft. Favor roles that value range, speed, problem solving, experimentation, AI adoption and hands-on execution.

## Priority roles
1. Creative Strategist / Senior Creative Strategist
2. AI Creative / AI Creative Strategist / Generative AI Creative
3. Creative Technologist when creative/prototyping oriented
4. Integrated Creative / Senior Creative
5. Creative Producer / AI Creative Producer
6. Brand Creative / Creative Strategy
7. Creative Lead, especially startup, scale-up, AI-native and hands-on teams
8. AI Content Creative / Creative Innovation
9. Conceptual Art Director when concept-led
10. Performance Creative Strategist when large-budget paid-media ownership is not a hard requirement

Stretch: Associate Creative Director, AI-focused ACD, startup Creative Director, compatible Head of Creative roles.

## Matching philosophy
Do not require 100% requirement matching. High fit can mean roughly 70% of the core work is already credible and the remaining gap is realistically learnable.

Prefer AI-native companies, creative technology companies, startups/scale-ups, modern remote agencies/studios, in-house creative teams, and product companies building new creative capabilities.

Remote is the primary objective. Prefer global, EMEA, and Europe remote opportunities, then strategically useful Milan/Italy and Istanbul/Turkey opportunities. Down-rank strict office-only roles outside target geographies.

## Positive signals
Increase fit for creative strategy, concept development, integrated campaigns, storytelling, treatments/storyboards, film/video/production, creative direction, pitching, brand strategy, cultural insight, generative AI, rapid prototyping, experimentation, creative automation, multidisciplinary collaboration, zero-to-one work, and creative systems/operations.

## Down-rank
Pure graphic designer, videographer, camera operator, video editor, social media manager, media buyer, software engineer, ML engineer, junior/intern/coordinator, highly specialist art-direction craft roles, and very senior agency leadership roles requiring long conventional agency history.

Judge responsibilities, not title alone.

## High-fit job output
Where supported, surface: fit reasons, biggest gap/risk, best evidence or portfolio angle, likely hiring-manager function, a specific outreach angle, and an application priority such as Apply now / Targeted outreach / Stretch / Skip.

Never invent experience, metrics, contacts, or project results.

## Evidence strategy
The candidate is stronger when evaluated through work and problem-solving evidence than title chronology alone. For valuable roles, prefer a small targeted proof such as a concise creative observation, relevant existing case, short teardown, campaign direction, or product/system demonstration. Do not default to large speculative pitch decks.

## Current optimization target
Qualified remote interview pipeline > number of scraped jobs > title prestige.

Keep the feed smaller and actionable rather than broad and noisy.
