# WebGPT Job Search Mandate — 2026-09-29

## Objective

The immediate objective is to maximize the probability of obtaining strong **remote interviews quickly**. Job Finder should inspect its own current search/scoring/ranking system and make the changes it considers necessary to serve this objective.

This note is an execution mandate, not a request for WebGPT to modify Job Finder's implementation. Job Finder owns the implementation decisions.

## Candidate positioning

Do not model the candidate only as a conventional Creative Director.

The strongest professional intersection is:

**creative thinking + strategy + visual/production execution + AI-native workflows + systems thinking**

The candidate is strongest in roles where conceptual thinking and strategy are combined with hands-on making, visual storytelling, modern AI workflows, rapid prototyping, and multidisciplinary execution.

## Primary target roles

Prioritize opportunities such as:

- Creative Strategist / Senior Creative Strategist
- AI Creative / Generative AI Creative / AI Creative Strategist
- Integrated Creative / Senior Creative
- Creative Producer / AI Creative Producer
- Creative Technologist, when the role is creative rather than software-engineering-heavy
- Brand Creative / Brand Creative Strategist
- Creative Innovation
- Creative Lead, especially hands-on startup, scale-up, or in-house roles
- AI Content Creative / AI-native creative roles
- Performance Creative Strategist where deep historical ownership of large paid-media budgets is not a hard requirement
- Conceptual Art Director where the emphasis is ideas and integrated creative thinking

Creative Director / Associate Creative Director and similar senior roles may be surfaced as **stretch opportunities**, but the pipeline should not depend on them.

## Work environment priority

Remote is the primary requirement.

Prefer, in broad order:

1. Global remote
2. EMEA remote
3. Europe-compatible remote
4. Contractor-friendly remote opportunities compatible with an internationally mobile candidate
5. Particularly strong Milan / Italy opportunities when relevant to the broader relocation direction

Do not over-prioritize office-dependent roles simply because their title matches.

## Professional strengths to match against

Relevant evidence and strengths include:

- Concept development and creative ideation
- Creative strategy and reframing business/brand problems
- Visual storytelling
- Film/video production knowledge
- Treatments, storyboards, narrative and pitch development
- Creative direction and multidisciplinary execution
- Working with designers, filmmakers, editors and other creative specialists
- AI-native creative workflows and experimentation
- Rapid prototyping and learning new tools quickly
- Connecting strategy, creative and execution rather than operating in only one layer
- Systems thinking, workflow design and AI orchestration
- Founder-level experience building a live AI creative product and its surrounding operating systems

## Gaps / roles to de-prioritize

Do not reject every role containing these areas, but reduce fit when success depends primarily on evidence the candidate does not yet strongly possess:

- Many years of traditional agency tenure as a hard requirement
- Proven ownership of very large paid-media budgets as the core qualification
- Pure software engineering / developer roles
- Pure graphic-design production roles
- Pure videographer/editor roles with little conceptual or strategic ownership
- Highly specialized craft roles where deep specialist tenure is the main hiring criterion
- Extremely senior ECD/CCO-style organizational leadership roles

Do not require a 100% qualification match. If the candidate can credibly perform the core job and the remaining gap is learnable, the opportunity can still be high value.

## Ranking philosophy

Optimize for **interview probability and role quality**, not raw job volume or title prestige.

A slightly unconventional role that strongly matches the candidate's creative + AI + systems profile can outrank a conventional Creative Director listing with weak evidence fit.

Where possible, evaluate strong opportunities with:

1. Why this candidate fits
2. Biggest evidence/experience gap
3. Which existing portfolio/case-study angle should be shown
4. Who the most relevant hiring decision-maker is, when discoverable through permitted sources
5. A concise direct-outreach angle
6. Whether the role is a primary-fit or stretch opportunity

## Operating principle

The candidate's career history is multidisciplinary and non-linear. Do not penalize this automatically. The goal is to find employers where breadth is useful: AI-native companies, startups/scale-ups, modern remote agencies, in-house creative teams, creative technology environments, and organizations looking for people who can move from idea and strategy into execution.

At the same time, do not inflate seniority or fabricate evidence. Match the candidate on demonstrated capabilities and clearly distinguish stretch opportunities.

## Privacy

Do not add private-life, sensitive, or unnecessary personal information to this public repository. This mandate intentionally contains only professional search context.

## Job Finder ownership

Job Finder should now review this mandate against its existing configuration, scoring, discovery lanes, sources and ranking logic, then independently implement the smallest effective changes needed to improve the remote-interview pipeline.
