# TEM Gold Standard — Can JobFinder

Reference role: **Brand & Creative Design Lead at tem**.

JobFinder should treat roles with this shape as top-priority opportunities even when the title differs.

## Core signals

- Senior creative / brand leadership while remaining hands-on
- Brand systems, visual identity, storytelling and multi-channel campaigns
- AI-first creative practice
- AI used for concepting, ideation, iteration, design or production
- Ownership or adoption of internal creative AI tooling
- Startup / scale-up / high-growth environment
- Cross-functional work with Brand, Product and Growth
- Remote-first / distributed work
- Strong craft plus fast execution

## High-priority title families

Brand & Creative Design Lead; Brand Design Lead; Brand Creative Lead; Brand Lead; Head of Creative; Creative Lead; Creative Director; Creative Strategy Lead; Creative Strategist; Creative Innovation Lead; Creative Technologist; AI Creative Lead; Generative AI Creative; Creative AI; Performance Creative Lead; Campaign Creative Lead.

## Discovery rule

Do not require an exact title match. A role should rise strongly when the description combines creative/brand ownership with AI-driven concepting or production, hands-on execution, and remote/startup signals.

## Regression test

The tem archetype must score as a standout/top-tier role. Any future search/scoring change that would suppress this archetype is a regression.

## Adjacent lane

Keep realistic routes open in performance/growth creative, marketing operations, creative operations, project/program management, client/customer success, account management, implementation and AI workflow roles, but rank TEM-style creative/AI leadership above generic adjacent roles.
